# AR4 Robotics — Algeria

**Pioneering industrial 6-axis robotic automation in Algeria.**

We are **Ayoub Chalabi** and **Ishak Benfiala** — engineering students at the Higher National School of Advanced Technologies (ENSTA), Algeria, and co-founders of an initiative to manufacture, customize, and deploy industrial 6-axis robot arms for manufacturing industries across Algeria.

> We are currently raising capital and technical partnerships to realize our first physical production prototype. We are actively calling on visionary investors, industrial partners, and technical advisors to join us in bringing this first prototype to life and accelerating robotic automation across Algeria's industrial landscape.

## The AR4 Robot Arm

An industry-proven, high-torque **6-axis articulated robot arm** engineered for precision, accessibility, and real manufacturing work — at a fraction of the cost of commercial systems.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Degrees of Freedom | 6 |
| Reach | 629 mm |
| Payload | 0.8 kg |
| Repeatability | ±0.2 mm |
| Weight | 12.5 kg |
| Manufacturing Cost (COGS) | ~$1,350 |
| Target MSRP | $3,499 *(vs $25k+ commercial arms)* |
| Gross Margin | 61.4% |

### Key Features
- **CNC-machined 6061-T6 aluminum** construction with harmonic belt transmission
- **Closed-loop stepper actuation** with optical encoder feedback (Teensy 4.1 controller)
- **Full CAD + software stack**: SolidWorks assemblies, URDF, ROS/ROS2 MoveIt, Isaac Sim, Gazebo, MuJoCo, RViz, ThreeJS web visualization, and computer vision
- **Open hardware** with no vendor lock-in — MIT-licensed ecosystem
- Ideal for CNC machine tending, pick-and-place, light assembly, packaging, quality inspection, and robotics R&D

### Who It's For
- Industrial machine shops seeking affordable CNC tending & automation
- Manufacturing plants scaling light assembly and inspection lines
- University research labs & engineering schools requiring open 6-DOF hardware
- Automation integrators wanting open hardware & ROS2 control
